How it works

Three steps. Your reviewer stays in charge.

We draft fields from the certificates you already have. We do not issue certificates, and we do not decide coverage.

  1. You send certificates from your records

    During a pilot, you email ACORD 25s (or a shared folder we agree on). This website does not accept uploads and does not store files.

  2. We extract the fields and flag anything uncertain

Benchmark

What we have actually tested.

Official ACORD 25 test on 22 fresh public certificates (12 digital, 10 scanned), November 22, 2026, 5:37 PM PT, using only free local tools — no paid AI APIs. The official score is the extractor with the correction step off. These are lab results on public forms, not a production guarantee on an agency’s own book.

An honest caveat on this set. After the first lock of these 22 certificates, two extractor rules were changed because of errors observed on this same set, then the set was re-run. The rules are general, but this official 22 is therefore not a held-out set that was never used for tuning. A clean holdout is being labeled for November 29, 2026. Scans remain the weak spot, which is why every flagged field — and every scan — goes to a person on our team before a draft is returned. FAQ

Questions, answered plainly.

Do you issue certificates or make coverage decisions?

No. We prepare COI drafts from your records, for approval by your designated reviewer. We don’t make coverage decisions, and we don’t issue certificates.

Do I upload certificates on this page?

No. This page is a static demo with precomputed results. It does not accept uploads, and it does not send certificates anywhere. A pilot starts by email to kfairley9999@gmail.com.

How accurate is it?

In the official test (corrections off, 22 public ACORD 25 certificates), critical-field accuracy was 82.2% (511 / 622) and overall field accuracy was 78.7% (609 / 774): 95.4% on digital PDFs and 61.5% on scans. 27.3% went straight through (6 of 22), and there was 1 leaked error in 22. After the first lock of this set, two extractor rules were changed because of errors on it, so these 22 are not a never-tuned holdout. Lab results on public forms are not a production guarantee on your book.

What happens with scans?

In the official test every scan was flagged for a person. During the pilot a person on our team reviews every draft.

Are there paying customers?

Not yet. This is a pre-launch back-office service for Los Angeles independent agencies, and the free pilot is how we measure results on your own book.
