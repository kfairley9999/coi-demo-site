Benchmark

What we have actually tested.

Official ACORD 25 test on 22 fresh public certificates (12 digital, 10 scanned), November 22, 2026, 5:37 PM PT, using only free local tools — no paid AI APIs. The official score is the extractor with the correction step OFF. Memory was trained only on earlier labeled data, not this set. These are lab results on public forms, not a production guarantee on an agency’s own book.

Official test

Corrections OFF  ·  22 certificates  ·  774 fields

Scans are the failure mode. Faint grid lines and single boxed letters still trip free OCR, which is why every flagged field — and every scan — goes to a person on our team before delivery. The free 30-day pilot is how we measure the same figures on an agency’s own book.
