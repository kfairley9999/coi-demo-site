Benchmark

What we have actually tested.

Two runs on 80 real, publicly posted ACORD 25 certificates, November 22, 2026, using only free local tools — no paid AI APIs. These are lab results on public forms, not a production guarantee on an agency’s own book.

Test 1

44 certificates  ·  1,039 fields

Field-level accuracy93.2% (968 / 1,039)
Dates and policy numbers98.5–99.3%
Dollar limits93.7%
Straight-through (clean, unflagged)36.4% (16 of 44)
Flagged for a person34.1% (15 of 44)
Critical error that slipped past the flags2.3% (1 of 44)

Test 2

36 certificates  ·  25 digital, 11 scanned  ·  1,282 fields

Field-level accuracy90.3% (1,158 / 1,282)
Digital PDFs100% (876 / 876)
Scanned copies69.5% (282 / 406)
Straight-through50% overall; 72% on digital
Flagged for a person50% (18 of 36); 100% of scans
Critical error that slipped past the flags0 of 36
Scans are the failure mode. Faint grid lines and single boxed letters still trip free OCR, which is why every flagged field — and every scan — goes to a person on our team before delivery. The free 30-day pilot is how we measure the same figures on an agency’s own book.
