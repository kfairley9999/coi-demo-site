Benchmark

What we have actually tested.

Official ACORD 25 test on 22 fresh public certificates (12 digital, 10 scanned), November 22, 2026, 5:37 PM PT, using only free local tools — no paid AI APIs. The official score is the extractor with the correction step OFF. Memory was trained only on earlier labeled data, not this set. These are lab results on public forms, not a production guarantee on an agency’s own book.

Official test

Corrections OFF · 22 certificates · 774 fields

Field-level accuracy78.7% (609 / 774)
Straight-through (clean, unflagged)27.3% (6 of 22)
Flagged for a person68.2% (15 of 22)
Silent errors1 of 22
95% CI (document bootstrap)69.3–87.6%

Scans are the failure mode. Faint grid lines and single boxed letters still trip free OCR, which is why every flagged field — and every scan — goes to a person on our team before delivery. The free 30-day pilot is how we measure the same figures on an agency’s own book.

FAQ

Questions, answered plainly.

Do I upload certificates on this page?

No. This page is a static demo with precomputed results. It does not accept uploads, and it does not send certificates anywhere.

How accurate is it?

In the official test (corrections OFF, 22 public certificates), field accuracy was 78.7%: 95.4% on digital PDFs and 61.5% on scans. 27.3% went straight through (6 of 22), and there was 1 silent error in 22.

What happens with scans?

In the official test every scan was flagged for a person. During the pilot a person on our team reviews every certificate.

Are there paying customers?

Not yet. This is a pre-launch back-office service, and the free pilot is how we measure results on your own book.
